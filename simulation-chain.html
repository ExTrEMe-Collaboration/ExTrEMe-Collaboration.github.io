--- title: Simulation Chain permalink: /simulation-chain/ eyebrow: Research infrastructure lead: A multi-stage pipeline that models relativistic heavy-ion collisions from the earliest collision geometry, through hydrodynamic expansion, to final-state particle spectra and correlations. ---

Pipeline overview

The framework models the collision through distinct physical stages, connecting microscopic dynamics with macroscopic collective expansion.

  1. 1

    Initial state & geometry

    TRENTo A phenomenological initial-condition generator implementing an effective generalized-mean ansatz for energy and entropy deposition in heavy-ion collisions.

    PYTHIA / Angantyr For baseline calculations without the assumption of a quark-gluon plasma (QGP) core, collisions are modeled with the Angantyr model, based on the PYTHIA Lund string fragmentation framework.

  2. 2

    Pre-equilibrium dynamics

    KoMPoST Bridges the early collision geometry with viscous hydrodynamics by evolving energy-momentum tensor perturbations Tμν using kinetic theory, providing consistent hydrodynamic initial conditions at the initialization time τhydro.

  3. 3

    Relativistic viscous hydrodynamics

    MUSIC A (3+1)D relativistic viscous hydrodynamics code that solves energy-momentum conservation together with relaxation equations for the shear-stress tensor πμν and the bulk viscous pressure Π. It evolves the expanding quark-gluon plasma until local temperatures drop below the pseudo-critical transition temperature, extracting a freeze-out hypersurface.

  4. 4

    Particlization

    iSS A Monte Carlo particle sampler implementing the Cooper–Frye formalism on the freeze-out surface, converting continuous fluid fields into individual hadrons with viscous corrections to the momentum distributions.

  5. 5

    Hadronic cascade & decays

    UrQMD Simulates microscopic hadronic transport — elastic and inelastic rescattering and resonance decays — in the dilute hadronic phase. The pipeline supports two modes:

    • Decays only — isolates freeze-out signatures from the subsequent hadronic evolution.
    • Decays and interactions — full transport, modeling hadronic medium effects.
  6. 6

    Analysis & model-to-data comparison

    oscarToHadrex ThirteenToHadrex Format converters producing ROOT analysis trees, from which we extract observables including anisotropic flow coefficients (vn), momentum-dependent flow fluctuations and scaled transverse-momentum spectra, and perform Bayesian model-to-data parameter estimation.
