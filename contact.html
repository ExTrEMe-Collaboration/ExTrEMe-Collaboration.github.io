Prospective students & postdoctoral researchers

We welcome inquiries from prospective graduate students (MSc / PhD) and postdoctoral researchers interested in heavy-ion phenomenology, relativistic hydrodynamics, initial conditions, transport theory, machine learning, and Bayesian inference in extreme matter physics.

Please reach out to the coordinator, or directly to individual faculty members at your institution of interest, with your CV and a brief description of your research interests. Funding opportunities are available through Brazilian agencies (such as FAPESP, CNPq and CAPES) as well as international exchange programs.
