Research scope

Following a nuclear collision, stage by stage

Our research spans the theoretical description and numerical simulation of matter under extreme conditions of temperature and density — from the first femtoseconds of a collision to the hadrons that reach the detectors.

  1. 01

    Initial conditions & pre-equilibrium

    Fluctuating nuclear geometry, non-conformal energy deposition, and pre-hydrodynamic kinetic equilibration before hydrodynamic thermalization.

  2. 02

    Relativistic viscous hydrodynamics

    (3+1)D relativistic fluid dynamics with shear and bulk viscosities, causality constraints, attractors, and novel formulations (BDNK, Israel–Stewart).

  3. 03

    Particlization & hadronic cascade

    Cooper–Frye freeze-out with viscous momentum corrections, coupled to microscopic transport (UrQMD) for hadronic rescattering and decays.

  4. 04

    Bayesian inference & emulation

    Constraining temperature-dependent QGP transport coefficients (η/s(T), ζ/s(T)), the equation of state, and initial-state parameters through Bayesian parameter estimation.

  5. 05

    Observables & model-to-data comparison

    Anisotropic flow (vn), transverse-momentum (pT) spectra shapes, flow fluctuations, vorticity rings and hyperon polarization at RHIC and the LHC.

    See how the stages connect

    Our multi-stage simulation chain: TRENTo, KoMPoST, MUSIC, iSS and UrQMD.
