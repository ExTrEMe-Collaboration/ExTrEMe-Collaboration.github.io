## Research Scope

Our research spans the theoretical description and numerical simulation of matter under extreme conditions of temperature and density:

- **Initial Conditions & Pre-equilibrium Dynamics**: Understanding fluctuating nuclear geometry, non-conformal energy deposition, and pre-hydrodynamic kinetic equilibration before hydrodynamic thermalization.
- **Relativistic Viscous Hydrodynamics**: Simulating (3+1)D relativistic fluid dynamics with shear and bulk viscosities, investigating causality constraints, attractors, and novel hydrodynamic formulations (BDNK, Israel-Stewart).
- **Particlization & Hadronic Cascade**: Applying Cooper-Frye freeze-out with viscous momentum corrections, coupled with microscopic transport models (UrQMD) to simulate hadronic rescattering and decays.
- **Bayesian Inference & Emulation**: Extracting temperature-dependent Quark-Gluon Plasma transport coefficients ($\eta/s(T)$, $\zeta/s(T)$), equation of state properties, and initial state parameters through Bayesian parameter estimation.
- **Experimental Observables & Model-to-Data Comparison**: Predicting and analyzing anisotropic flow ($v_n$), transverse-momentum ($p_T$) spectra shapes, flow fluctuations, vorticity rings, and hyperon polarization in collisions at RHIC and the LHC.
